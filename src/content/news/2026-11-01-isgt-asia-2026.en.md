From October 30 to November 1, lab members attended the 2026 IEEE PES Innovative Smart Grid Technologies Asia Conference (IEEE PES ISGT Asia) in Wuhan, Hubei Province, and presented a poster for the paper "A method for generating extreme scenarios and achieving rapid response across multiple time scales for park clusters based on GPD-CGAN".

Addressing the insufficient samples for extreme scenarios in park clusters and the difficulty of multi-park coordinated response, the paper proposes an extreme-scenario generation and multi-time-scale rapid-response method based on the Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD) and the Conditional Generative Adversarial Network (CGAN): it defines three types of extreme quantitative indicators—high-power, low-power, and ramp events—uses the GPD exceedance-threshold model to fit the sample tail and quantify extreme probabilities, and combines CGAN to accurately generate joint wind–solar–load output scenarios. Building on this, a "scenario–response" coupled coordination architecture is constructed, introducing the main-grid regulation signal and an inter-park power mutual-aid mechanism, and an energy-storage fine-control model with event-direction awareness is designed to achieve nested multi-time-scale cluster coordination. Case studies show that the generated joint wind–solar–load output scenarios effectively reflect historical extreme characteristics and meet the requirements of graded assessment and rapid regulation response for extreme distribution-network scenarios.
