On July 30, 2026, the CSG (China Southern Power Grid) Energy Storage Industry Cluster Symposium was held in Panyu, Guangzhou. Prof. Mingjian Cui from the School of Electrical and Information Engineering, Tianjin University, was invited to deliver a talk titled "Optimized Dispatch of Wind–Solar–Storage–Hydrogen Microgrids/Clusters and Distributed Storage under Multi-Agent Interests," sharing insights on the forecasting–dispatch–coordination chain and multi-agent distributed storage, drawing on the multidisciplinary practice of Tianjin University's national energy-storage platform.
